Guidelines For Parents

KIDZEE Pai layout operates for 5 days a week, from Monday to Friday.

Parents and guardians are not allowed to visit children or facilitators during class hours. Please approach the Center Head in the office for emergencies.

CCTV is for security purposes; daily viewing is not allowed. In an emergency, submit a written application to view footage during 'after school hours'.

For transfers to another KIDZEE center, submit a duly filled Transfer Form (available in the Almanac) to the Center Head.

The Leaving Certificate and Milestones will only be issued upon application and clearance of all outstanding fees.

Tuition Fees must be paid in advance per term. Fees are non-refundable even if the child leaves immediately after admission.

Encourage wards to be regular and punctual. Inform the center of any long absences.

Do not send wards to school if they are suffering from any ailment. Absence of >2 weeks without info leads to admission withdrawal.

Send children in neat uniforms and school shoes (Mandatory for Playgroup, Nursery, JKG, SKG).

PG & Nursery wear uniforms Mon/Wed/Fri. KG children wear uniforms all 5 days and on special occasions.

Send extra set of clothes, undergarments, and a hand towel daily. Provide a fresh handkerchief (do not pin to dress).

Show the Escort Card at dispersal; without it, the child will not be allowed to leave.

Check the WhatsApp Broadcast group daily for notes. Observations will be updated on the Kidzee app.

Parents are requested to wear formal attire when visiting school premises.

Label every item (bag, lunch box, water bottle, etc.).

Send healthy, nutritious vegetarian food. Avoid junk food. Non-veg is not allowed. Send appropriate quantities; overfeeding causes discomfort.

Syllabus provided during culmination. Please recapitulate at home playfully, without stress.

No Paper-Pencil Exams! Assessment is through Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation (CCE).

Milestones shared on Culmination Meet days. Both parents are advised to attend Parent Teacher Meetings.

Submit a medical fitness certificate if absent for >2 days due to illness.

Do not wear expensive gold/silver ornaments. School is not responsible for misplacement.

No feeding bottles; use proper water bottles/sippers. No diapers after settlement period; cooperate in toilet training.

Send a clean napkin daily. Parents should not handhold children during home assignments.

General Guidelines

If a student is suffering from cold, cough or fever, please don't send them to school till they recover.

Parents/Guardians are not expected to visit their child in classrooms during operating hours unless invited.

Notebooks and textbooks should be neatly covered with transparent covers, labeled, and submitted within a week.

Student's belongings (lunch bag, notebooks, shoes) must be labeled with the student's name to avoid confusion.

Ensure your child does not carry valuable items (smartwatches, jewelry). Management is not responsible for loss.

Parents should ensure the child dedicates quality time to completing home assignments and projects.

Transport Policies

Vehicle and driver details will be shared with parents.

Do not communicate with the driver directly. Contact the Center Head anytime (+91 8904456611).

Students must be on-time at the pick point. The van will not wait as it affects other students.

Transport fee must be paid prior to availing the service.

Inform the person picking up the student at drop-time. ID proof must be provided to the driver/caretaker.

Communicate any changes (leave, withdrawal) directly to the Center Head.
